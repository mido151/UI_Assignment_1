About the Event

TechFest 2026 brings students together to explore new ideas in computing, artificial intelligence, robotics, and digital innovation. Discover student projects, learn from technical presentations, and connect with other technology enthusiasts.

Whether you enjoy coding, engineering, or creative problem solving, TechFest offers an opportunity to learn and share ideas.
